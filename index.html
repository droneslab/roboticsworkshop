Workshop Overview

The SUNY RAISE (Robotics, Autonomy & Intelligent Systems in Engineering) workshop aims to bring together faculty, researchers, and industry professionals from across New York State to develop a strategic roadmap for advancing education, research, and engagement in robotics and embodied intelligence.

This workshop will foster collaboration, identify key challenges and opportunities, and establish a vibrant community to position New York as a leader in robotics innovation.

Workshop Objectives

  • Establish a SUNY-wide community of robotics researchers and educators
  • Identify key research thrusts and educational pathways
  • Develop collaborative research proposals and educational programs
  • Engage industry partners for workforce development and technology transfer
  • Create a strategic roadmap for SUNY's role in robotics advancement

Target Audience

  • Faculty and researchers from SUNY institutions
  • Industry professionals in robotics and automation
  • Government representatives and policymakers
  • Graduate students in robotics-related fields
  • Educational leaders and curriculum developers

Expected Outcomes

  • A comprehensive SUNY Robotics Roadmap document
  • Formation of specialized working groups for key research areas
  • Preliminary proposals for collaborative research projects
  • Framework for SUNY-wide robotics curriculum development
  • Industry-academia partnership agreements
  • Plans for future workshops and conferences
